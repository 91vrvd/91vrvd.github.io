Earth Day Map and Earth Night Map (8K), downloaded 2026-10-01.
8K JPEG files are unchanged. 4K versions were downsampled and JPEG-compressed.
The source combines and adjusts NASA imagery, including Blue Marble.
These are static composite textures, not real-time satellite imagery.
Real-time day/night illumination is calculated separately by this website.

Original download URLs:
